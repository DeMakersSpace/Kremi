## Design Context

### Users
Small-business owners at De Makers Space who need to update Kremi seasonal crepes without editing code. They want to upload product photos, descriptions, and publish the changes to the live GitHub-backed site.

### Brand Personality
Warm, polished, practical. The tone should feel premium but approachable, with a food-first presentation that keeps the product and story front and center.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing Kremi visual language: cream, red, matcha green, Japanese dessert editorial styling, strong typography, and clear hierarchy. New admin surfaces should be simple, calm, and efficient rather than flashy.

### Design Principles
- Make the upload flow obvious and low-friction.
- Keep token and repo controls visible but secondary to content entry.
- Preserve Kremi's brand palette and typography.
- Show the seasonal menu as a clean, food-led section with strong imagery.
- Prefer clear state feedback and safe publishing steps over cleverness.
